Quick Answers
How do I open an account?▾
Click "Open Account" at the top of the page, fill in your details, and verify your email. Approval for personal accounts is instant.
How long do transfers take?▾
Local transfers are typically instant. International transfers can take 1-3 business days depending on the destination and currency.
Is my money safe with RejoiceFuture?▾
Yes. Your funds and data are protected with 256-bit encryption, multi-factor authentication, and continuous fraud monitoring.
My card isn't working — what do I do?▾
Sign in and freeze the card immediately from the Cards tab, then send us a message below describing the issue so we can help further.
I already have an account — where do I get support?▾
Sign in and open the Support tab in your dashboard to start a ticket and message our team directly.
